This work reveals and analyzes the perception bottleneck in vision-language models (VLMs) when dealing with chart understanding tasks. The research identifies key limitations in how current VLMs process and interpret visual information in charts and graphs.

The paper provides insights into the architectural and training limitations that cause these perception bottlenecks, and proposes potential solutions for improving chart understanding capabilities in future VLM architectures.
