C-Eval is a comprehensive evaluation suite designed to assess foundation models on Chinese language understanding across multiple disciplines and difficulty levels. The paper presents a carefully curated benchmark covering various academic subjects and cognitive levels.

This work provides important insights into the capabilities and limitations of current foundation models in Chinese language processing, establishing a standardized framework for evaluating model performance across diverse domains.
