This groundbreaking research discovers the existence of a universal truthfulness hyperplane inside large language models. The paper demonstrates that truthfulness in LLMs can be represented as a geometric structure within the model's internal representations.

The work provides new insights into how LLMs process and generate truthful information, opening up new avenues for improving model reliability and interpretability. This discovery has significant implications for understanding and controlling the truthfulness of language model outputs.
