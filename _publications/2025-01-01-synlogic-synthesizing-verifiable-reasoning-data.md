SynLogic presents a novel framework for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale. This work focuses on learning logical reasoning capabilities in language models through carefully constructed training data that can be automatically verified for correctness.

The paper introduces methods for generating large-scale reasoning datasets with guaranteed correctness, enabling more effective training of language models on logical reasoning tasks. This approach addresses key challenges in reasoning research by providing a scalable solution for creating high-quality training data.
